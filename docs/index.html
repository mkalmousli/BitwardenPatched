FAQ

Is this official?

No. It is an unofficial community fork of the open-source Bitwarden clients and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Bitwarden Inc.

Which APK do I need?

Most phones since 2017 need arm64-v8a; the button above picks for you. Older 32-bit phones: armeabi-v7a. Emulators and Chromebooks: x86_64.

Does it replace the official app?

No. The app id is com.x8bit.bitwarden.patched, so both can be installed side by side. You log in with your normal account.

What exactly is patched?

The client-side premium check is always on, plus name, app id and fork badge. Encryption, networking and sync are untouched. Anything the server enforces still follows your plan. Full patch list.

Is it allowed?

The clients are GPL-3.0, so modifying and redistributing them is permitted. Bitwarden's hosted service has its own terms; self-hosted servers are the intended setup. If you rely on Bitwarden's cloud, consider buying a plan.

Why can't Chrome add it in one click?

Chrome, Brave and Edge only install extensions from their own stores, or unpacked in developer mode. The steps above take about a minute. Firefox installs the signed add-on directly.
